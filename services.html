What You Get

I provide comprehensive local SEO that includes everything your business needs to show up when local customers are searching.

Here's what that means in practice:

Google Business Profile Optimization

Your Google Business Profile is often the first thing potential customers see. I optimize every element: categories, business description, photos, posts, attributes, and more. The goal is to get you showing up in the map pack for relevant searches in your area.

Citation Building and Management

Citations are mentions of your business name, address, and phone number across the web. Search engines use these to verify your business and determine your local authority. I build citations on relevant directories and platforms, fix inconsistencies, and make sure your business information is accurate everywhere it appears.

On-Page SEO

If your website needs optimization work, I handle it. Title tags, meta descriptions, header structure, content optimization, internal linking. Whatever needs to be done to help search engines understand what you offer and who you serve.

Monthly Reporting

You get clear, easy-to-understand monthly reports. No fifteen-page documents filled with charts that don't mean anything. Just the information you need to see that progress is being made and customers are finding you.

What Gets Added Based on Your Needs

Some businesses need review generation strategies. Some need local link building. Some need both. Some need neither. I assess what your business actually needs and include it. The pricing reflects the scope of work, which is why there's a range rather than a single number.

Pricing: $500 - $1,000 per month

The range depends on what your business needs and how competitive your market is. A home services business in a crowded market will require more work than a specialty B&B with little competition. We'll discuss the specific scope and pricing in our initial conversation.

The First Three Months

The first three months are the most intensive. That's when the foundation gets built: fixing issues, optimizing profiles, building citations, getting everything in place. This is where most of the heavy lifting happens.

By the end of those three months, you should be seeing increased customer activity. If you're not, I keep working at no additional charge until you do. You don't pay for results you're not getting.

What This Service Isn't

This isn't magic. It does take some time, and it does take ongoing maintenance to stay ahead of the competition biting at your heels.

So, I shouldn't manage social media; you'd be better at that being so close to your business. And I won't write blog posts every week. Instead, I'll focus on what moves the needle for getting folks contacting you.

Restated, what I do is local SEO. If you need those other things, there are people who do them well, but often times content itself is best created by you, the business.

How We Start

We begin with a conversation about your business. What you do, who your customers are, what your local competition looks like. From there, I can tell you what needs to be done and what the monthly investment will be.

No contracts. No long-term commitments. If at any point you want to stop, you stop. The work continues because it's working, not because you're locked in.
